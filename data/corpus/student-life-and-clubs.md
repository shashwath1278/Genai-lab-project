# Student Life, Clubs and Fests

## Clubs
Northgate has more than 40 student clubs, grouped under the Dean of Student Welfare. Students can join clubs at the Club Fair held in the second week of the odd semester. Popular clubs include:

- **Technical**: Coding Club (competitive programming and hackathons), Robotics Club, Northgate Racing (Formula Student car), Aero Club (drones), Open Source Society, Cybersecurity Club, IEEE and ACM student chapters.
- **Cultural**: Music Club, Dance Crew, Drama Society (English and Kannada theatre), Photography Club, Literary and Debate Society, Film Club.
- **Social**: NSS unit, Rotaract Club, Green Campus Club, blood donation drives.
- **Entrepreneurship**: E-Cell, which runs pitch competitions and connects students with the Incubation Centre.

Starting a new club needs 25 student members and a faculty advisor; proposals go to the Dean of Student Welfare.

## Fests
- **Zenith** is the annual technical fest, held in November, with hackathons, robotics competitions, project expos and talks. It draws participants from over 100 colleges.
- **Utsav** is the cultural fest, held in March, with music, dance, drama, fashion show and pro-nights.
- **Sports Week** is held in February, with inter-department tournaments.

## Sports
Students can play cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, throwball, kabaddi, badminton, table tennis, chess and carrom. Northgate teams take part in inter-college and university tournaments every year. Students selected for college teams get attendance support for matches and practice.

## NCC and NSS
Northgate has an NCC unit (Army wing) and an NSS unit. NSS volunteers complete 120 hours of community service over two years and receive a certificate. NCC cadets can earn B and C certificates.

## Student council
The student council has elected class representatives and nominated club leaders. It meets the Principal once a month and helps organise fests, the orientation week and hostel committees.
