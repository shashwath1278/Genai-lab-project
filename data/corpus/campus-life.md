# Clubs, Fests, Sports and Start-ups

## Clubs
Cultural: Indian Music Team, Western Music Team, Lasya (dance), Prayaag (cultural and literary), Theatrix (drama), Chiraranga (Kannada theatre). Literary: TNT, DEBSOC (debate), Quiz Club. Technical: CodeRIT, Google Developers Club, Tensor AI, SecuRIT (cybersecurity), Aion, AWS Club, QuantumRIT. Engineering teams: Aero Club (Team Editha, Team Quatlas) and SAE Club (Team Stier Racing, Team Velocita, Team Volante).

## Fests and culture
Udbhav is the flagship cultural platform, run with the Department of Extra-curricular and Co-curricular Activities (DECA).

## Sports
Cricket, football, basketball, badminton, athletics and indoor games, with basketball and tennis courts and a modern gymnasium. Annual events: the M. S. Ramaiah Memorial Cricket and Basketball Tournaments, VTU inter-collegiate tournaments and the Annual Athletic Meet. The sports page says top sportspersons can get free first-year B.E. or MBA admission under management seats.

## NSS
Flagship events: M S Ramaiah Memorial Blood Donation Camp, rural and slum visits, health and hygiene drives, and educational support.

## Start-ups
Ramaiah Evolute (launched March 2021) is the innovation and start-up platform; the Entrepreneurship Development Cell dates from July 2003.

## International
Students take part in SAE Aero Design West and the UAV competition at Maryland (USA), and fellowships such as MITACS (Canada), DAAD (Germany) and UTSIP (Japan). Collaborations include Illinois (Urbana-Champaign and Chicago), Texas A&M and the University of Sydney. Source: MSRIT brochure 2026.
