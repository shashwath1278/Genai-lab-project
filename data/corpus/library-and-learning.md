# Library and E-Learning

## Library
Six libraries covering 1,762 square metres, with 1,17,000 volumes, 38,500+ titles, 23,900+ e-books and 7,800+ full-text e-journals, remote access to digital resources and digital workstations. Opening hours and borrowing rules aren't in the published brochure. Source: MSRIT brochure 2026.

## Smart classrooms and e-learning
Smart classrooms and an E-Learning Centre. The IMPARTUS lecture recording platform has over 250 recorded courses for on-demand viewing.
