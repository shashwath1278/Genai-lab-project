# Campus Facilities

## Academic buildings and labs
The campus has three academic blocks (A, B and C) with 92 classrooms, all with projectors and smart boards. There are 64 laboratories, including a VLSI design lab, an EV powertrain lab, an additive manufacturing (3D printing) centre, a material testing lab, a cloud computing lab and a GPU cluster for AI and research work.

## Innovation Centre
The Innovation Centre is open 24x7 to students with project approval. It has a maker space with laser cutters, 3D printers, electronics workbenches and soldering stations, and is home to the Incubation Centre and the Training & Placement Cell.

## Internet and IT
The whole campus, including hostels, is covered by Wi-Fi. Each student gets a Northgate email account, access to the learning management system (LMS) and licensed software such as MATLAB and CAD tools. The IT Helpdesk handles login, Wi-Fi and portal issues.

## Food
There is a central food court with five counters (South Indian, North Indian, Chinese, juices and snacks, and a bakery), open from 7:30 am to 9:00 pm. Two smaller cafés operate near the library and Block C. Prices are fixed in consultation with the student council.

## Sports facilities
Outdoor: a cricket ground, a football field, two basketball courts, two volleyball courts and a throwball court. Indoor: an indoor sports hall with four badminton courts, table tennis tables and a chess and carrom room. A gym with cardio and weight equipment is open to all students from 6:00 to 9:00 am and 4:00 to 8:00 pm.

## Health Centre
The Health Centre, next to Hostel Block H2, has a doctor on campus from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm, a nurse 24x7, a small pharmacy and two observation beds. An ambulance is available 24x7, and a multi-speciality hospital about 4 km away is tied up with the institute. All students are covered by group health insurance of Rs 1 lakh per year, included in the annual fees.

## Other facilities
- A bank branch with two ATMs on campus.
- A stationery and photocopy shop.
- An auditorium with 1,200 seats and two seminar halls.
- A counselling room at the Student Centre (see the student support document).
- Ramps, lifts and accessible washrooms in all academic blocks.
