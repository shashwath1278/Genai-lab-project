# College Bus Transport

## Routes and coverage
Northgate runs 38 college buses on 24 routes covering most parts of Bengaluru, including Yelahanka, Hebbal, Malleshwaram, Rajajinagar, Jayanagar, Whitefield, Electronic City and Banashankari. The full route map with stop timings is published on the student portal and the transport app, which also shows live bus location.

## Timings
Buses reach campus by 8:15 am for 8:30 am classes. Evening buses leave campus at 4:45 pm and 6:15 pm (the later bus is for students with labs, clubs or sports). On Saturdays the return bus leaves at 1:15 pm.

## Bus pass
Transport is optional. Students opt in when they pay fees, and the fee depends on the distance zone of their boarding point (Zone A, B or C; see the "Hostel, Mess & Transport Fees" document). The bus pass is valid for the whole academic year and is linked to the student ID card. Route changes are allowed at the start of each semester if seats are available.

## Rules
Students must carry their ID card on the bus. Seats are not reserved, except the first two rows, which are kept for women students and faculty. Complaints about delays or driver behaviour can be raised on the transport app or with the Transport Office at the main gate.

## Parking
Students who drive can register two-wheelers for free parking. Four-wheeler parking for students is limited to final-year students and costs Rs 3,000 per year.
