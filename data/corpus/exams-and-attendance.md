# Examinations, Attendance and Student Support

## Examinations
MSRIT is autonomous and conducts its own examinations: Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) plus a Semester End Examination (SEE), on an automated system. VTU awards the degree. Results: exam.msrit.edu. Contact: Controller of Examinations, coe@msrit.edu, 080-23600822 extn 376. Source: www.msrit.edu/examination.html. Grading scale, revaluation fees and the 2026-27 exam timetable aren't on the pages used here; ask the Controller of Examinations.

## Attendance
The minimum is 85% in each subject, and 40% in internal marks, as per VTU norms and the autonomy regulations. Progress reports go to parents at 45 and 90 days into the semester. Source: www.msrit.edu/support/proctorial-system.html.

## Proctors and counselling
Each student has a proctor. A clinical psychologist is on campus on Fridays, 2 to 5 pm; register through your proctor or head of department.

## Ragging
Ragging is a crime. Report it to grievances@msrit.edu or 080-23608445 (Registrar Administration).
