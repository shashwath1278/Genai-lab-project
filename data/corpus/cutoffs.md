# Cutoff Ranks: KCET and COMEDK

## How to read these
These closing ranks come from admission portals, not from MSRIT itself: check the KEA and COMEDK allotment lists before deciding. They are for General Merit. Cutoffs for SC, ST, Category-1, 2A, 2B, 3A and 3B aren't included here; KEA publishes them at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in.
