# Central Library

## Timings
The Central Library is open **8:00 am to 10:00 pm, Monday to Saturday**, and 9:00 am to 5:00 pm on Sundays. During semester-end exams, the reading room stays open 24 hours. The library is closed on national holidays.

## Collection
The library holds about 85,000 printed books, 140 print journals and magazines, and a digital library with access to major engineering and management journal databases, e-books and previous years' question papers. Digital resources can be used from anywhere on campus Wi-Fi and from home through the remote-access login on the student portal.

## Borrowing rules
- Undergraduate students can borrow **4 books** at a time for **14 days**.
- Postgraduate students can borrow 6 books for 21 days.
- Faculty can borrow 10 books for a semester.
- Books can be renewed twice online if no one else has reserved them.
- Reference books, journals and question-paper collections are for reading inside the library only.

## Fines
The fine for late return is Rs 2 per day per book for the first week and Rs 5 per day after that. Lost books must be replaced with the same edition or paid for at twice the current price. Students with unpaid fines cannot get a no-dues certificate.

## Facilities
The library has a 300-seat reading room, 12 discussion rooms that can be booked for group study, 60 computers, printing and scanning (Rs 1 per page for black and white), and a quiet zone on the first floor. A book bank lends a full set of first-year textbooks for the year to students receiving need-based support.
