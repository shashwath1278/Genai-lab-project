# Examinations, Grading and Results

## Evaluation pattern
Each theory course is evaluated through Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) for 50 marks and a Semester End Examination (SEE) for 50 marks. CIE includes two internal tests (30 marks, average of both), assignments or quizzes (10 marks) and a course project or seminar (10 marks). Lab courses are evaluated on lab records, a lab test and a viva.

To pass a course, a student needs at least 40% in the SEE and at least 40% overall (CIE + SEE combined). Students need a minimum of 20 out of 50 in CIE to be allowed to write the SEE.

## Grading
Northgate uses a 10-point grading system:

| Marks | Grade | Grade points |
|---|---|---|
| 90-100 | O (Outstanding) | 10 |
| 80-89 | A+ | 9 |
| 70-79 | A | 8 |
| 60-69 | B+ | 7 |
| 55-59 | B | 6 |
| 50-54 | C | 5 |
| 40-49 | P (Pass) | 4 |
| Below 40 | F (Fail) | 0 |

SGPA is calculated for each semester and CGPA across all semesters. A CGPA of 7.75 or above with no failed courses earns "First Class with Distinction"; 6.75 to 7.74 is "First Class".

## Exam registration and hall tickets
Students register for semester-end exams on the portal and pay the exam fee by the deadline in the academic calendar. A late fee of Rs 500 applies for one week after the deadline. Hall tickets are issued only to students with at least 85% attendance in each course and no fee dues.

## Make-up exams and supplementary exams
Students who miss an internal test for a medical reason or an approved college event can take one make-up test per course with supporting documents. Students who fail a course can write the supplementary exam held in July, or re-register for the course in a later semester.

## Results, revaluation and photocopies
Results are published on the portal about three weeks after the last SEE. Students can request a photocopy of their answer script within 5 days of results (Rs 400 per course) and apply for revaluation within 7 days (Rs 800 per course). If revaluation changes the marks by 10% or more, the revaluation fee is refunded.

## Academic integrity
Use of unfair means in exams, including carrying phones or smartwatches into the hall, leads to cancellation of that exam and may lead to cancellation of all exams in that semester. Repeated cases are referred to the Disciplinary Committee.
