# About Northgate Institute of Technology

## Overview
Northgate Institute of Technology (NGIT) is an autonomous engineering and management institute in North Bengaluru, Karnataka. It was founded in 2004 by the Northgate Education Trust and today has about 4,200 students, 260 faculty members and nine programmes across undergraduate engineering, management and computer applications.

The campus covers 42 acres, with separate hostels for men and women, a central library, an innovation centre and sports grounds. The nearest metro station is about 6 km away, and college buses cover most of Bengaluru.

## Vision and mission
NGIT's vision is "engineers who build for people". The mission focuses on three things: hands-on learning from the first semester, close links with industry through projects and internships, and a supportive campus where every student has a faculty mentor.

## Academic structure
NGIT follows a choice-based credit system with two semesters a year (odd semester from August to December, even semester from January to May). Undergraduate engineering degrees take four years (eight semesters). MBA, MCA and M.Tech take two years (four semesters).

Every B.E. student completes a mini-project in Year 2, an internship of at least eight weeks before Year 4, and a final-year capstone project. Students can earn a minor (for example Robotics, Data Science or Entrepreneurship) by taking 18 extra credits.

## Programmes offered
Undergraduate (B.E.): Computer Science & Engineering, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Information Science & Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering and Civil Engineering.

Postgraduate: MBA, MCA and M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering. Detailed eligibility, cutoffs and fees are in the "Programmes, Eligibility & Fees" document.

## Leadership
The institute is led by the Principal, Dr. Shalini Venkatesh, supported by the Dean of Academics, the Dean of Student Welfare and the Controller of Examinations. Each department is headed by a Head of Department (HoD).

## Quality and feedback
NGIT's programmes are reviewed every year by an Academic Council with industry members. Student feedback on every course is collected each semester and published in summary form on the student portal.
