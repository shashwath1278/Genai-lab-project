# Undergraduate (B.E.) Admissions Guide

## Admission routes
There are four ways to join a B.E. programme at Northgate:

1. **KCET (Government quota)**: seats allotted through Karnataka CET counselling. Fees are the government-quota fee.
2. **COMEDK**: seats allotted through COMEDK UGET counselling.
3. **JEE Main (Management quota, JEE merit)**: management seats filled in order of JEE Main percentile.
4. **Management quota (direct)**: remaining management seats, filled on the basis of 12th marks and an interview.

Closing ranks from the previous year and the fee for each route are listed in the "Programmes, Eligibility & Fees" document. Cutoffs change every year depending on demand, so last year's closing rank is only a guide.

## Basic eligibility
Candidates must have passed 12th (PUC) or equivalent with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with one of Chemistry, Computer Science, Electronics or Biology. The minimum aggregate in these subjects is 45% for General Merit candidates and 40% for OBC and SC/ST candidates.

Candidates appearing for their 12th exams this year can apply provisionally; admission is confirmed after results.

## Step-by-step: KCET and COMEDK students
1. Take part in KCET or COMEDK counselling and choose Northgate in your option entry.
2. Download your seat allotment order once a seat is allotted.
3. Pay the counselling-authority fee online as instructed by KEA or COMEDK.
4. Report to the Northgate Admissions Office within the reporting window with original documents (see the "Admission Documents Checklist").
5. Pay the Northgate admission fee and the balance of the first-year fee at the Accounts Section.
6. Collect your student ID, portal login and orientation schedule.

## Step-by-step: Management quota and JEE merit
1. Fill the online application form on the Northgate admissions portal. Applications for the 2027 intake open on 15 January 2027 and close on 31 May 2027.
2. Upload 10th and 12th marks cards and, if applicable, your JEE Main scorecard.
3. Attend a short interaction with a faculty panel (online or on campus).
4. If selected, you receive a provisional admission letter by email.
5. Pay the admission fee within 15 days to confirm the seat, then complete document verification.

JEE Main applicants are ranked by percentile; candidates with 98 percentile and above are considered for the JEE Excellence Scholarship automatically.

## Lateral entry for diploma holders
Diploma holders in a relevant engineering branch with at least 45% (40% for reserved categories) can join directly into the second year (third semester) through DCET counselling. About 10% of seats in each B.E. programme are set aside for lateral entry. Lateral-entry students pay the yearly fee of their admission route for three years.

## Change of branch
Students may request a change of branch at the end of the first year if they have a CGPA of 8.5 or above, no backlogs, and a seat is vacant in the requested programme. Requests go to the Dean of Academics in June.

## Campus visits
Prospective students and parents can visit the campus Monday to Saturday between 10:00 and 16:00. Guided tours run at 11:00 and 15:00 and can be booked through the Admissions Office. A campus open day for applicants is held every April.
