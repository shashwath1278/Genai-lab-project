# B.E. and B.Arch Admissions

## Eligibility for B.E.
10+2 (PUC or equivalent) with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with one of Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science or Electronics. Minimum aggregate: 45% for General Merit, 40% for SC, ST, Category-1 and OBC candidates. Source: www.msrit.edu/admissions.html.

## Admission routes
- KCET through the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) counselling; MSRIT's KEA college code is E006 (as listed by FindUrCollege).
- COMEDK UGET through COMEDK counselling.
- Management quota: apply to the Admissions Section. A rank card of any one entrance test (KEA, COMEDK or JEE) is required. JEE Main is not a separate route: the rank card counts as the entrance test for a management seat.
- Supernumerary quota for NRI, PIO and foreign nationals (B.E. only).
- Lateral entry to the third semester through the KEA diploma quota (DCET), with 45% in the diploma for General Merit and 40% for SC/ST/OBC.
Management quota eligibility follows KCET guidelines. For 2026-27 the website announced that admissions under the management, NRI, PIO and foreign national quotas were open.

## Eligibility for B.Arch
50% in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics and 50% aggregate at 10+2, with English as a language, and a qualifying NATA score (the website states at least 40%).

## How to apply
KCET or COMEDK: register for counselling, choose MSRIT in your option entry, download the allotment order, then report to the Admissions Section with your documents and pay the first-year fee. Management or NRI: contact the Admissions Section at admn@msrit.edu or 080-23607902.
