# Scholarships at MSRIT

## Schemes
Post-Metric Scholarship for SC/ST students; fee reimbursement for SC/ST candidates; fee concession for Category I, II and III students; extra boarding and lodging allowance for Category I, II and III; National Scholarship Portal schemes for minority students; Defence Scholarship; MSRIT Alumni Scholarship (Alumni Section); and merit scholarships from private and public organisations.

## How to apply
Applications are available at the Admission and Scholarship Section, in September each year (one list on the website says August). Amounts are set by each scheme. Government schemes: ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in and scholarships.gov.in. Source: MSRIT brochure 2026 and www.msrit.edu/admissions.html.

## Fee waiver seats
Supernumerary quota (SNQ) seats under CET have a first-year fee of Rs 52,820 in 2026-27.
