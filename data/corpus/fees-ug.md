# B.E. and B.Arch Fees 2026-27

## First-year fees for 2026 admissions
| Seat | B.E., Karnataka | B.E., non-Karnataka / SAARC | B.Arch, Karnataka | B.Arch, non-Karnataka / SAARC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KCET (CET) | Rs 1,40,820 | Rs 1,42,620 | Rs 1,42,570 | Rs 1,44,370 |
| COMEDK or management quota | Rs 3,54,820 | Rs 3,56,620 | Rs 3,56,570 | Rs 3,58,370 |
| CET supernumerary quota (SNQ) | Rs 52,820 | | | |

The fee depends on the seat, not the branch: every B.E. branch has the same CET fee and the same COMEDK/management fee in this notice. Foreign national, PIO and NRI students also pay a miscellaneous fee of 600 US dollars. Source: MSRIT notice, 1st year fee structure 2026-27.

## Second to final year fees in 2026-27
| Year (batch) | CET regular | COMEDK / management | CET SNQ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2nd year (2025 batch) | Rs 1,26,020 | Rs 3,25,320 | Rs 44,220 |
| 3rd year (2024 batch) | Rs 1,20,355 | Rs 3,05,697 | Rs 44,220 |
| 4th year B.E. (2023 batch) | Rs 1,14,434 | Rs 2,82,926 | Rs 45,220 |
| 3rd year lateral entry (2025 batch) | Rs 1,26,020 | Rs 1,26,020 | |

Final-year fees include the alumni association fee. The registration and start of the odd semester (III, V, VII, IX) was 24 August 2026 and the last date to pay was 22 August 2026. Source: MSRIT notice, UG higher semester fee structure 2026-27.

## NRI and PIO fees (students already admitted, per year in 2026-27)
For the 2025-26 and 2024-25 batches: CSE 8,600 US dollars; ISE, CSE (AI & ML), CSE (Cyber Security), AI & ML and AI & DS 6,600; ECE 4,600; Civil, Mechanical, EEE, Chemical, IEM, Instrumentation, Telecommunication, Medical Electronics and Biotechnology 3,600.

## How to pay
First year: demand draft in favour of "M.S. Ramaiah Institute of Technology" payable at Bangalore, or net banking, RTGS, IMPS or UPI. Higher semesters pay online at www.msrit.edu.

## Not published
Hostel, mess, transport and caution-deposit amounts aren't in the published notices. Ask the hostel office (080-23600003) or the Admissions Section.
