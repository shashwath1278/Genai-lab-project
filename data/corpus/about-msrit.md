# About M S Ramaiah Institute of Technology

## Overview
M S Ramaiah Institute of Technology (MSRIT), also called Ramaiah Institute of Technology (RIT), is a self-financing engineering college in Bengaluru, established in 1962 by Dr. M. S. Ramaiah through the Gokula Education Foundation. It is an autonomous institute affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), which awards the degrees, and is approved by AICTE and the Government of Karnataka. Academic autonomy was granted in 2007. It offers 18 undergraduate and 15 postgraduate programmes, plus research degrees. Source: MSRIT brochure 2026.

## Rankings and accreditation
In NIRF 2025 (Ministry of Education), MSRIT is ranked 75 among 1,584 engineering institutes and 31 among 131 architecture institutes, and is in the 101-150 band overall. It is accredited by NAAC with an A+ grade (score 3.28), and all eligible engineering programmes are accredited by NBA. Source: MSRIT brochure 2026 and www.msrit.edu. (One heading on the website still says "A grade"; the brochure and body text say A+.)

## Faculty and research
The faculty-student ratio is 1:15 and over 70% of faculty hold a PhD. The brochure reports more than Rs 10 crore of external research funding in three years (Rs 13 crore+ on the research page), 40+ patents a year and 1,000+ publications a year. Centres of Excellence: Imaging Technologies, Advanced Materials Technology, Cyber Physical Systems, Antennas and Radio Frequency Systems, Bio and Energy Materials Innovation, and the Schneider Centre for Building Automation.

## Vision and mission
Vision: to be an institution of international eminence, renowned for imparting quality technical education, cutting edge research and innovation to meet global socio-economic needs. Motto: "Devotion to Enlightenment".
