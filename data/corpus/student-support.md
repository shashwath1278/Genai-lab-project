# Student Support, Mentoring and Grievances

## Faculty mentors
Every student is assigned a faculty mentor at admission, with about 20 students per mentor. Mentors meet their mentees at least twice a semester, track attendance and marks, and are the first point of contact for academic or personal concerns. Parents can contact the mentor directly; mentor details are on the student portal.

## Counselling
Two full-time counsellors are available at the Student Centre, Monday to Saturday, 9:30 am to 5:30 pm. Sessions are free and confidential and can be booked on the portal or by walking in. Outside these hours, students can call the 24x7 wellbeing helpline listed on the back of their ID card.

## Academic support
- Remedial classes are held for first-year Mathematics and Physics for students who need extra help.
- Peer tutoring is run by senior students, coordinated by each department.
- Students with backlogs get a study plan from their mentor before supplementary exams.

## Students with disabilities
Students with disabilities can apply to the Dean of Student Welfare for support such as extra exam time, a scribe, accessible classrooms or hostel rooms on the ground floor. A disability certificate is required.

## Grievance redressal
Students can raise academic or administrative grievances through the online grievance portal. Each grievance gets a tracking number and a response within 7 working days. Unresolved grievances go to the Grievance Redressal Committee, which meets every month. Complaints about ragging and harassment follow the separate process in the conduct policy.

## Information for parents
Parents receive a parent-portal login at admission, where they can see attendance, internal marks, results, fee receipts and hostel leave records. A parent-teacher meeting is held once every semester after the first internal tests.
