# Placements and Training

## Figures published by MSRIT
The 2026 brochure states: 90% placements, 1,300+ offers, 250+ companies and 50+ international placements. The website's quick facts (no year given) show 358 companies visited, 1,892 job offers, a highest package of Rs 50 LPA and an average of Rs 7.66 LPA. These don't match each other: the website text dates the 1,892 offers to the 2016 batch (by 378 companies), so treat the brochure as the more recent.

## Not published
Branch-wise placement rates and median pay aren't on the pages used here. NIRF's yearly data submission is the place to check median salary. Third-party sites quote figures from Rs 50 to 58 LPA for the highest package in 2025-26; those aren't official.
