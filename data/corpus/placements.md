# Placements and Internships (2025 season)

## Overview
The Training & Placement Cell organises campus recruitment, internships and training. In the 2025 placement season, 186 companies visited campus and **87%** of eligible students across all programmes received at least one offer. Branch-wise placement rates and median and highest packages are listed in the "Programmes, Eligibility & Fees" document.

## Who recruits
Recruiters include software product companies, IT services firms, semiconductor and electronics companies, automotive and EV manufacturers, construction and infrastructure firms, banks, consulting firms and start-ups. Some of the regular recruiters are Cobalt Systems, Vertex Analytics, Larkspur Semiconductors, Orbit Mobility, Granite Infra, Bluewave Bank and Kestrel Consulting.

## Eligibility for placements
Students must have a CGPA of at least 6.0 with no active backlogs to register for campus placements. Some companies set higher cutoffs (often 7.0 or 7.5). A student who accepts an offer can apply to only one more company, and only if its package is at least 1.5 times the accepted offer ("dream offer" rule).

## Training
Placement training starts in the fifth semester and covers aptitude, coding, communication and mock interviews. Training is included in the annual fees; there is no separate charge. Students also get access to an online coding practice platform from the first year.

## Internships
All B.E. students complete at least eight weeks of internship before the final year, usually during the summer after the sixth semester. ISE students can opt for a full-semester industry internship in Year 4. MBA students do an eight-week summer internship after the first year, and MCA students do a six-month internship in the final semester. About 60% of interns receive pre-placement offers.

## Higher studies and entrepreneurship
Around 9% of graduates go on to higher studies in India or abroad each year. The Northgate Incubation Centre supports student start-ups with mentoring, office space and seed grants of up to Rs 5 lakh.
