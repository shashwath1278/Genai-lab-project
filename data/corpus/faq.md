# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is hostel accommodation compulsory?
No. Hostel is optional for all students. About 55% of students live in the hostels; the rest commute, most of them using college buses.

## Can I get a seat if I miss the counselling reporting date?
Seats allotted through KCET or COMEDK must be reported within the window set by the counselling authority. Northgate cannot extend this deadline. Contact the Admissions Office immediately if you face a problem.

## Is there a uniform?
There is no uniform. Students follow a smart-casual dress code, with lab coats and safety shoes in labs and workshops.

## Can students work part-time or do freelance projects?
Students may take part-time or freelance work outside class hours as long as attendance stays above 85%. Paid internships during semesters need approval from the HoD.

## Are laptops required?
A laptop is strongly recommended from the first year for B.E., MCA and M.Tech students (any recent laptop with at least 8 GB RAM). Students without a laptop can use the computer labs and the library computers.

## Do you offer evening or weekend courses?
No. All programmes at Northgate are full-time, on-campus programmes.

## Can parents track their child's progress?
Yes. Parents get a parent-portal login with attendance, marks, results and fee details, and receive monthly attendance summaries.

## Is there a mobile app?
Yes. The Northgate app has the timetable, attendance, results, hostel leave, bus tracking and campus announcements.

## How do I get a bonafide certificate or transcript?
Request it on the student portal. Bonafide certificates are issued within 2 working days; transcripts take about 10 working days and cost Rs 500 per copy.

## Does the college have an ATM or bank?
Yes, there is a bank branch with two ATMs on campus.
