# Hostel and Mess

## Hostels
MSRIT has separate hostels for boys and girls, accommodating up to 1,500 students, with a common vegetarian mess, hot water, TV lounges and a health centre (brochure 2026). Boys' blocks: Aryabhatta Block, Ratan Tata Block, Fresher's Block for first-years, and the Post Graduate Boys Hostel at Devasandra. Girls' hostel details, capacity per block and hostel fees aren't published in the sources used here; contact hostel@msrit.edu or 080-23600003 / 7829134223.

## Rooms and facilities (boys' hostel)
Single and double rooms with attached or common bathrooms and hot water; a cot, chair, almirah and reading table each (bring your own mattress and lock). Free Wi-Fi, free laundry (outfits only), ironing at subsidised rates, RO drinking water, recreation halls with LED TV, indoor and outdoor games, a night canteen up to 11 pm, guest rooms for visiting parents on payment, CCTV in all blocks and round-the-clock security with extra staff at the Fresher's Block.

## Mess timings
Breakfast 7:30 to 9:30 am, lunch 12:00 to 2:15 pm, snacks and tea 4:30 to 5:30 pm, dinner 7:30 to 9:30 pm, all days. The mess is vegetarian, with north and south Indian food decided by a student mess committee. No room service, and no mess leave except for emergencies with proof.

## Health
A health centre with a senior doctor is open Monday to Saturday, 5 to 7 pm; medicines are free. Emergencies go to Ramaiah Hospital nearby.

## Rules
Silence after 10 pm. Cooking, outside food deliveries (Swiggy, Zomato) in rooms, irons, kettles, heating coils and speakers are not allowed. Alcohol, drugs, smoking and non-vegetarian food are banned. Inform the warden if you'll be away over 24 hours. Room changes need the warden's permission; keeping two rooms or overstaying costs Rs 300 a day. Hostel and mess deposits, room rent and other charges are paid in full for the academic year on joining.

## Refund if you vacate
| When | Refund |
|---|---|
| Before occupying the room | 100% (Rs 1,000 admission fee deducted) |
| Within one month of joining | 75% of rent and maintenance, plus balance mess advance and security deposit |
| Within two months | 50% of rent and maintenance, plus balance mess advance and security deposit |
| After two months | Security deposit only |

Source: MSRIT boys' hostel guide and www.msrit.edu/facilities/hostel.html.
