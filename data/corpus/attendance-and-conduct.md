# Attendance, Code of Conduct and Anti-Ragging Policy

## Attendance requirement
Students must maintain at least **85% attendance in every course** to be eligible for the semester-end exams. Students between 75% and 85% may be allowed to write exams on medical grounds or for approved events (sports, hackathons, NSS) with supporting documents, at the Principal's discretion. Below 75%, the student must repeat the course.

Attendance is recorded on the portal after every class, and parents receive a monthly attendance summary by SMS and email. Mentors contact parents when a student's attendance drops below 80%.

## Code of conduct
- Students must carry their ID card on campus at all times.
- The dress code is smart casual. Shorts, sleeveless tops and slippers are not allowed in classrooms and labs. Lab coats and safety shoes are compulsory in workshops and labs where specified.
- Phones must be on silent in classrooms and the library, and are not allowed in exam halls.
- The campus is a no-smoking, no-alcohol and no-drugs zone.
- Political campaigning and posters are not allowed on campus.

## Anti-ragging policy
Northgate has **zero tolerance** for ragging. Ragging includes any teasing, bullying, harassment or forcing a student to do something against their will, whether in person or online. Punishments range from suspension and cancellation of hostel accommodation to expulsion and a police complaint.

All students and parents sign an anti-ragging undertaking at admission. An Anti-Ragging Committee and an Anti-Ragging Squad conduct surprise checks in hostels, buses and canteens, especially during the first semester.

## How to report
Students can report ragging or harassment 24x7 on the anti-ragging helpline, by email, through the anonymous form on the student portal, or to any faculty mentor or warden. Complaints are handled confidentially, and action starts within 24 hours.

## Internal Complaints Committee
An Internal Complaints Committee handles complaints of sexual harassment. It is chaired by a senior woman faculty member and includes an external member. Complaints can be made in writing or by email, and the identity of the complainant is protected.
