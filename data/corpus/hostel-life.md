# Hostel Life, Rules and Mess

## Hostel blocks
Northgate has four hostel blocks on campus: H1 and H2 for men, H3 and H4 for women, with about 1,100 beds in total. Room types range from triple sharing to single AC rooms; room fees and seat counts are listed in the "Hostel, Mess & Transport Fees" document. First-year students are usually placed in double or triple rooms; single AC rooms are offered to final-year students first.

Every room has a bed, mattress, study table, chair, wardrobe and a ceiling fan. All blocks have 24x7 Wi-Fi, RO drinking water, solar-heated hot water from 5:30 to 9:30 every morning, power backup, a common room with TV and indoor games, a reading room and a washing-machine laundry room on each floor.

## Room allotment
Hostel applications for the next academic year open in May through the student portal. Rooms are allotted in this order of priority: students living more than 50 km away, first-year students, and then others by date of application. Room-change requests are accepted only in the first two weeks of each semester.

## Hostel timings and leave
- Hostel in-time is **10:00 pm** on weekdays and **11:00 pm** on Fridays and Saturdays, for all blocks.
- Students staying out overnight or going home must apply for leave on the hostel app at least 12 hours in advance. For first-year students, a parent must approve the leave request on the app.
- Late entry is recorded and parents are informed by SMS after three late entries in a month.

## Visitors
Parents and guardians registered at admission can visit on any day between 9:00 am and 7:00 pm in the visitors' lounge. Other visitors need the warden's permission. Visitors are not allowed inside rooms. Parents can stay in the campus guest house (subject to availability) at Rs 1,200 per night.

## Mess and food
Each hostel block has its own mess run by the hostel committee with a professional caterer. Students choose either the vegetarian plan or the mixed plan (vegetarian on most days, non-vegetarian three days a week) when they apply; mess fees are in the fees document. The plan can be changed once per semester.

Meal timings: breakfast 7:00 to 9:00, lunch 12:30 to 2:00, snacks 5:00 to 6:00, dinner 7:30 to 9:30. The weekly menu is decided with a student mess committee and posted on the hostel app. Special diets for medical reasons (for example diabetic or gluten-free meals) are available with a doctor's note.

## Rules
- Alcohol, smoking, drugs and cooking appliances (except electric kettles) are not allowed.
- Ragging in any form leads to immediate expulsion from the hostel and disciplinary action.
- Students are responsible for any damage to hostel property in their rooms.
- Quiet hours are from 11:00 pm to 6:00 am.

## Safety and health
All hostel blocks have CCTV in common areas, biometric entry and round-the-clock security. Women's hostels have a resident lady warden and female security staff. The campus Health Centre is next to Hostel Block H2, with a doctor on campus from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm and an ambulance available 24x7.
