# Fee Payment, Instalments and Refund Policy

## How to pay
Fees can be paid online through the student portal (UPI, net banking, debit or credit card) or by demand draft in favour of "Northgate Institute of Technology", payable at Bengaluru. Cash is not accepted. Card payments above Rs 1 lakh carry a 1% gateway charge; UPI and net banking are free.

Receipts are generated instantly on the portal and can be downloaded at any time.

## Due dates
- First-year fees are paid at the time of admission.
- For later years, the annual tuition fee is due by 15 July each year.
- Hostel and mess fees are due before the hostel opens for the academic year.

A late fee of Rs 100 per day applies after the due date, up to a maximum of Rs 5,000. Students with fees pending beyond 60 days cannot register for semester-end exams.

## Instalments
Tuition fees from the second year onwards may be paid in two instalments (July and December) on request to the Accounts Section, with an instalment processing charge of Rs 1,000. Hostel fees can also be split into two instalments. First-year fees cannot be paid in instalments.

## Education loans
Northgate provides fee structure letters and bonafide certificates for education loans within two working days. The institute has tie-ups with several banks that offer loans without collateral up to Rs 7.5 lakh for Northgate students. Loan disbursements can be paid directly to the institute's account.

## Refund policy
Refunds of tuition fees for cancelled admissions follow these rules:
- Cancellation **before classes begin**: full refund minus a processing fee of Rs 5,000.
- Cancellation **within 15 days of the start of classes**: 80% of the tuition fee is refunded.
- Cancellation **after 15 days**: no tuition refund, unless the seat is filled by another candidate, in which case the fee is refunded minus Rs 5,000.

The admission fee is non-refundable in all cases. Caution deposits (Rs 10,000 institute and Rs 15,000 hostel) are refunded within 45 days of course completion after a no-dues clearance. For KCET and COMEDK seats, the counselling authority's own cancellation rules also apply.

Refund requests are submitted to the Accounts Section with the original fee receipts and a cancellation form signed by the student and a parent.
