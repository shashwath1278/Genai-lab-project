# Programmes Offered at MSRIT

## Undergraduate programmes (18)
Four-year B.E. in Aerospace Engineering; Artificial Intelligence & Data Science; Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning; Biotechnology; Chemical Engineering; Civil Engineering; Computer Science & Engineering; CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning); CSE (Cyber Security); Electrical & Electronics; Electronics & Communication; Electronics & Instrumentation; Electronics & Telecommunication; Industrial Engineering & Management; Information Science & Engineering; Mechanical; Medical Electronics. Plus the five-year B.Arch.

## Postgraduate programmes (15)
MBA and MCA (two years each). M.Tech in Aerospace Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Biotechnology, Computer Network Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Data Sciences, Digital Electronics & Communication, IOT and Sensor Systems, Robotics & Artificial Intelligence, Structural Engineering, and VLSI Design & Embedded Systems. M.Arch in Advanced Architecture and Landscape Architecture.

## Research degrees
PhD and MS (by research) in Architecture, Biotechnology, Chemical, Civil, CSE, EEE, ECE, ETE, EIE, IEM, ISE, Mechanical, Medical Electronics, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, MBA and MCA.
